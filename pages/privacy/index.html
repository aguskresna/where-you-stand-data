Privacy policy

Where you stand, for Android and iOS. Effective 11 November 2026.

The app collects no personal data. Everything you type (your income, age, birthday, name or anything else) stays on your phone. The comparisons are calculated on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

What the app downloads

To compare you with official statistics, the app downloads public data files (for example income percentiles or life tables) from aguskresna-data.pages.dev. These files are the same for everyone; the app never sends what you typed to get them.

The files are hosted by Cloudflare. As with any website, Cloudflare's servers receive your device's IP address and basic request details (such as the app's network library and the file requested) in order to deliver the files and protect the service. We don't receive those details, keep them or use them, and no analytics are switched on. Cloudflare's handling is described in its privacy policy.

What is stored on your phone

The downloaded data files are kept on your phone so the app works offline. What you type is not saved after you leave a question. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Sharing a result

If you tap Share, the app makes a picture of the result and hands it to the app you pick (such as a messaging app). It goes only where you send it; we never receive it.

What the app doesn't have

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, crash reporting, tracking or session recording.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Where the figures come from

The statistics come from public sources such as the US Census Bureau, the National Center for Health Statistics, the UN, the World Bank, OpenDOSM and BPS. Each result names its sources. No agency endorses the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/aguskresna/where-you-stand-data/issues.
